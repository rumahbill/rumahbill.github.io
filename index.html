How it works

  1. Add your tenants onceName, WhatsApp number, rent and due day. That's all.
  2. Remind in one tapRumahBill picks the right message and fills in the name, amount and date. It opens in your own WhatsApp.
  3. Mark who has paidSee who is paid, due or late this month, send a receipt, and keep a record month by month.

Built to be safe

Your records stay with youTenant details are saved on your phone, not on our servers. Save a backup file any time.
RumahBill never touches your moneyTenants pay you the way they do today. RumahBill only keeps the record.
Nothing is sent without youEvery message opens in your own WhatsApp, and you press send.
