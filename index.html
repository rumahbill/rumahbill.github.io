What is RumahBill?

RumahBill is a rent and bill helper for shared homes. Housemates add their rent and utility bills, split them fairly, pay each other with DuitNow in their own banking app, and keep one clear record of who has paid.

Today, many landlords still chase rent and bills one message at a time. In this trial, we start with you. Save your tenants' numbers once, pick a ready-made message, and WhatsApp opens with the message already written for each tenant.

For landlords

Save tenants, send rent and bill reminders on WhatsApp, and keep a journal of who has paid each month. This is what you can use in the trial.

For tenants

Split bills, pay by DuitNow and keep one shared record. This part comes later.
